The '''2024 Grand Slam of Darts''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2024 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that was held at Aldersley Leisure Village in [[Wolverhampton]], England from 9–17 November 2024.<ref>{{cite web |last=Phillips |first=Josh |date=11 September 2023 |title=2024 PDC calendar unveiled: New destinations & all midweek PCs confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2023/09/11/2024-pdc-calendar-unveiled-new-destinations-all-midweek-pcs-confirmed |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231017071058/https://www.pdc.tv/news/2023/09/11/2024-pdc-calendar-unveiled-new-destinations-all-midweek-pcs-confirmed |url-status=dead |archive-date=October 17, 2023 |website=[[Professional Darts Corporation]] |access-date=11 November 2023}}</ref> It was the eighteenth staging of the [[Grand Slam of Darts]] organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]].

[[Luke Humphries]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Rob Cross]] 16–8 in the [[2023 Grand Slam of Darts|2023 final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=19 November 2023 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Luke Humphries beats Rob Cross 16-8 to win final |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/67470522 |website=BBC Sport |access-date=3 March 2025}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Dave |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231120120531/https://www.pdc.tv/news/2023/11/19/unstoppable-humphries-races-mr-vegas-grand-slam-darts-title |url-status=dead |archive-date=November 20, 2023 |website=PDC |access-date=20 November 2023 |date=19 November 2023}}</ref> He was eliminated in the group stage, after losing his first two matches.<ref>{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=11 [[Michael van Gerwen]] was eliminated in the group stage for the first time since [[2010 Grand Slam of Darts|2010]],<ref>{{cite web |last=Wellens |first=Megan |date=13 November 2024 |title=Grand Slam [[Luke Littler]] won the tournament, defeating [[Martin Lukeman]] 16–3 in the final for his first ranking major title.<ref name=BBCF>{{cite web |last=Matthews |first=Callum |date=17 November 2024 |title=Littler wins Grand Slam to go fifth in world rankings |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/c4g72n4d1kno |access-date=17 November 2024 |work=BBC Sport}}</ref>

==Qualification==
The qualifying criteria for the Grand Slam were as follows:<ref>{{cite web |date=23 August 2024 |title=2024 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts qualification latest |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/august/23/2024-mr-vegas-grand-slam-darts-qualification-latest |website=www.pdc.tv |access-date=23 Aug 2024}}</ref>

* Finalists from PDC televised events over the previous twelve months (up to a maximum of 16 players),
* Additional qualifiers (up to a maximum of 8 players),
* European Tour events winners (to fill up to 24 players),
* Players Championship event winners (to fill up to 24 players)

The qualifiers were:<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=3 November 2024 |title=2024 Mr Vegas Grand Slam of Darts field confirmed |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/november/03/2024-mr-vegas-grand-slam-darts-field-confirmed |publisher=[[Professional Darts Corporation]] |access-date=3 November 2024}}</ref>
