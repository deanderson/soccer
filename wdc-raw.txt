The '''2025 PDC World Darts Championship''' (known for sponsorship reasons as the '''2024/25 Paddy Power World Darts Championship''') was a professional [[darts]] tournament that was held from 15 December 2024 to 3 January 2025 at [[Alexandra Palace]] in London, England. It was the thirty-second [[PDC World Darts Championship|World Darts Championship]] to be organised by the [[Professional Darts Corporation]] and the eighteenth to be held at Alexandra Palace. The winner received £500,000 from a total prize fund of £2,500,000.

The tournament featured 96 players, with the top 32 highest ranked players on the [[PDC Order of Merit]] being [[Seeding (sports)|seeded]] through to the second round. [[Luke Humphries]] was the defending champion, having defeated [[Luke Littler]] 7–4 in the [[2024 PDC World Darts Championship|2024]] final.<ref name=HumphriesGuardian24WCH>{{Cite web |last=Liew |first=Jonathan |date=3 January 2024 |title=Luke Humphries ends Luke Littler's fairytale in epic PDC world darts final |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/jan/03/luke-humphries-luke-littler-pdc-world-darts-championship-final |access-date=18 October 2024 |work=The Guardian}}</ref> However, Humphries lost 4–1 to [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]] in the fourth round.

In his 3–1 second-round victory over [[Ryan Meikle]], Littler set a new record for the highest [[High dart average|three-dart set average]] in a World Championship match, with 140.91 in the final set. Littler went on to beat [[Nathan Aspinall]] 5–2 in the quarter-finals and [[Stephen Bunting]] 6–1 in the semi-finals to reach a second consecutive final, where he defeated [[Michael van Gerwen]] 7–3 to win his first world title. Aged 17 years and 347 days, Littler became the youngest world champion in darts history. He also won the ''Ballon d'Art'' trophy for scoring the most 180s in the tournament, with 76.

[[Christian Kist]] and [[Damon Heta]] both hit [[nine-dart finish]]es at the tournament, with both players earning a £60,000 bonus.

==Overview==
The 2025 PDC World Darts Championship was the 32nd [[World Darts Championship]] organised by the Professional Darts Corporation, and the 18th to be held at [[Alexandra Palace]] in London, England. It took place from 15 December 2024 to 3 January 2025. The championship featured 96 participants, with the top 32 highest ranked players on the [[PDC Order of Merit]] being [[seeding (sports)|seeded]] through to the second round. ===Format===

All matches were played as straight in (player begins scoring with their first throw no matter what section is hit), double out (a segment on the outer ring or the [[Bullseye (target)|bullseye]]), requiring the players to score 501 points to win a [[Leg (darts)|leg]], finishing on a double. The matches were played in the [[Set (darts)|set]] format, with a minimum of three sets required to win a match.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Jenkins |first=Keith |date=3 January 2025 |title=PDC World Darts Championship: Format, rules, schedule, more |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/darts/story/_/id/42935593/world-darts-championship-101-format-rules-schedule-more |access-date=5 January 2025 |website=ESPN}}</ref> The following rules are observed:

* All sets were played to the best of five legs in the first round, and also in non-deciding sets of subsequent rounds.

* In the deciding set of all but the first round, the first player to win at least three legs and be leading by two or more won the set and the match. If the set reached a 5–5 tie without a winner, it was decided by a sudden-death leg with no throw for the bull.<ref name=SLifeFormat/>

===Ranking===
The rankings on the [[PDC Order of Merit]] are calculated on a two-year basis. Players who participated in the [[2023 PDC World Darts Championship]] were defending their prize money from that event on their ranking. At the end of the tournament, the PDC Order of Merit prize money from that event was deleted from their ranking. After the tournament, the top 64 in the PDC Order of Merit received a one-year extension on their tour card. Players in the final year of their tour card, or that do not hold a tour card for the 2024 season, who finished the tournament outside the top 64 lost their tour card, unless players inside the top 64 resign their tour card, which [[Steve Beaton]] did when he retired from the professional game.<ref>{{cite web |title=PDC Rankings |url=http://globaldarts.de/globalDartsEN/global/Rankingpdc.html |website=Global Darts |access-date=18 October 2024}}</ref>

===International qualifiers===
[[Niko Springer]] and [[Keane Barry]] qualified from the [[2024 PDC Development Tour series]], while [[2012 BDO World Darts Championship|2012 BDO World Champion]] [[Christian Kist]] and [[Alexander Merkx]] qualified from the [[2024 PDC Challenge Tour series]].<ref name=Field/>

[[Fallon Sherrock]], the only woman to have won a match at the PDC World Darts Championship, and [[Noa-Lynn van Leuven]] qualified from the [[2024 PDC Women's Series]], with Van Leuven becoming the first transgender competitor to play at the event. As with the previous edition, no player was eligible to compete in the 2025 PDC World Championship had they played at the 2024 WDF World Championship which concluded a week earlier. [[Beau Greaves]], the winner of the [[2024 Women's World Matchplay]], qualified but opted to play the WDF event.<ref>{{cite web |title=Greaves says no to PDC Worlds after Matchplay win |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/darts/articles/c0dmgl481ylo |publisher=[[BBC Sport]] |access-date=19 October 2024 |date=21 July 2024}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |date=12 November 2024 |title=Grand Slam of Darts: Beau Greaves open to playing men regularly and says she has gone 'stale' dominating women's game |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13252895/grand-slam-of-darts-beau-greaves-open-to-playing-men-regularly-and-says-she-has-gone-stale-dominating-womens-game |work=Sky Sports}}</ref>

[[Rashad Sweeting]] became the first player from the Bahamas to play at the PDC World Championship after sealing his qualification through the Championship Darts Latin America and Caribbean Order of Merit, while [[Stefan Bellmont]] became the first Swiss player to play at the PDC World Championship.<ref name=Bellmont>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Kieran |date=16 November 2024 |title=Stefan Bellmont makes history as first ever Swiss qualifier for PDC World Darts Championship |url=https://dartsnews.com/pdc/stefan-bellmont-makes-history-as-first-ever-swiss-qualifier-for-pdc-world-darts-championship |access-date=16 November 2024 |website=Dartsnews.com}}</ref>

The final places were awarded by a qualifier for non-qualified PDC Tour Card holders, with Rhys Griffin, [[Jeffrey de Zwaan]], [[Dylan Slevin]] and [[Matt Campbell (darts player)|Matt Campbell]] progressing through the qualifier.<ref>{{cite web |last=Gorton |first=Josh |date=25 November 2024 |title=Campbell, De Zwaan, Slevin, Griffin secure last Ally Pally spots |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/november/25/campbell-de-zwaan-slevin-griffin-secure-last-ally-pally-spots |website=[[Professional Darts Corporation|PDC]].tv}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=25 November 2024 |title=World Darts Championship: Simon Whitlock, Steve Beaton miss out in Tour Card Holder Qualifier for Alexandra Palace |url=https://www.skysports.com/darts/news/12288/13260522/world-darts-championship-simon-whitlock-steve-beaton-miss-out-in-tour-card-holder-qualifier-for-alexandra-palace |work=Sky Sports |access-date=30 November 2024}}</ref>

Debutants from the international qualifiers were Ryusei Azemoto, [[Stefan Bellmont]], [[:de:Joe Comito|Joe Comito]], Kai Gotthardt, Romeo Grbavac, [[Rhys Griffin]], [[:de:Lok Yin Lee|Lok Yin Lee]], [[Alexander Merkx]], [[Niko Springer]], [[Rashad Sweeting]], [[Alexis Toylo]] and [[Noa-Lynn van Leuven]].<ref name=Debutants/>

==Summary==
[[File:Michael Smith 6-3 Steve Lennon - Michael Smith - 2019249215712 2019-09-06 PDC European Darts Matchplay - 1108 - B70I6125 (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|Former champion [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] was the highest seeded player to be eliminated in his first match.]]

===Opening rounds===
The tournament began on 15 December, with defending champion [[Luke Humphries]] defeating Frenchman [[Thibault Tricole]] 3–0 in his opening match.<ref>{{cite web |date=15 December 2024 |title=World Darts Championship 2025 results: Luke Humphries beats Thibault Tricole to reach third round |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cq5l41j5vljo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref> Second seed and former champion [[Michael Smith (darts player)|Michael Smith]] became the highest-ranked player to fail to make it through the second round, losing 3–2 to [[Kevin Doets]].<ref>{{cite web |date=19 December 2024 |title=Michael Smith stunned by Kevin Doets in World Darts Championship upset |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/dec/19/michael-smith-stunned-kevin-doets-world-darts-championship-upset |website=The Guardian |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref> Smith was one of four former world champions not to win their opening match, being joined by [[Rob Cross]], who lost 3–1 to [[Scott Williams (darts player)|Scott Williams]],<ref name="RecordSeeds"/> [[Gary Anderson (darts player)|Gary Anderson]] who was beaten 3–0 by [[Jeffrey de Graaf]]<ref name="BBCAnderson">{{cite web |last=Keogh |first=Frank |date=22 December 2024 |title=Gary Anderson knocked out of PDC World Championship by Jeffrey de Graaf |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/c791x85737do |website=BBC Sport}}</ref> and [[Raymond van Barneveld]] who lost 3–1 to [[Nick Kenny (darts player)|Nick Kenny]].<ref name=BBC140>{{cite web |last=Keogh |first=Frank |date=21 December 2024|title=Luke Littler hits PDC World Championship record set average of 140 in win over Ryan Meikle |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cgq1lpyn24lo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref>

Two major champions from the 2024 season also failed to win a match, [[2024 World Grand Prix (darts)|2024 World Grand Prix]] winner [[Mike De Decker]] being beaten by [[Luke Woodhouse]]<ref name=BBCDeDecker>{{cite web |date=17 December 2024 |title=De Decker stunned by Woodhouse at World Championship |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cq5l8d537ego |website=BBC Sport |access-date=7 January 2025}}</ref> and [[2024 European Championship (darts)|2024 European Champion]] [[Ritchie Edhouse]] losing 3–1 to [[Ian White (darts player)|Ian White]],<ref name="BBCAnderson"/> with White himself having received a walkover victory over Sandro Eric Sosing in the first round after the Filipino withdrew with ill health.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Gorton |first1=Josh |date=22 December 2024 |title=Update: Sandro Eric Sosing |url=https://www.pdc.tv/news/2024/december/22/update-sandro-eric-sosing |website=PDC |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref>

In total, 14 seeds were eliminated in the second round, a record for the tournament; in addition to Michael Smith, Cross, Anderson, Van Barneveld, De Decker and Edhouse, [[Dave Chisnall]], [[Danny Noppert]], [[James Wade]], [[Ross Smith (darts player)|Ross Smith]], [[Martin Schindler]], [[Dirk van Duijvenbode]], [[Gabriel Clemens]] and [[Gian van Veen]] all lost their first game.<ref name="RecordSeeds">{{cite web |date=23 December 2024 |last1=Colman |first1=Jonty |last2=Matthews |first2=Callum |title=PDC World Darts Championship 2025 results: Rob Cross knocked out as record number of seeds fall |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cvg612k9d6ko |website=BBC Sport |access-date=31 December 2024}}</ref>

Former world champions to win their opening match, in addition to Humphries, were [[Michael van Gerwen]] who defeated [[James Hurrell]] 3–0, [[Peter Wright (darts player)|Peter Wright]] who knocked out [[Wesley Plaisier]] 3–1 and [[Gerwyn Price]] who beat [[Keane Barry]] 3–0. The highest average of the tournament, was thrown by [[Callan Rydz]] in his first round victory over Romeo Grbavac, hitting a [[High dart average|three dart average]] of 107.06; Rydz would go on to defeat [[Martin Schindler]] to make the third round.<ref>{{cite web |last=Conrad |first=Alex |date=29 December 2024 |title=Rydz explains how rival's pre-match comments backfired in spectacular fashion |url=https://talksport.com/darts/2426410/callan-rydz-dimitri-van-den-bergh-world-darts-championship/ |website=talkSPORT |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref> 

In his first round defeat to [[Madars Razma]], [[Christian Kist]] hit the first [[nine-dart finish]] at the World Championship since the [[2023 PDC World Darts Championship|2023 tournament's]] final.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Keogh |first1=Frank |date=18 December 2024|title=PDC World Darts Championship 2025 results: Christian Kist hits nine-darter but beaten |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cz9g3928l2lo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=31 December 2024}}</ref>

[[Callan Rydz]] continued to average highly in the third round, averaging 105.31 in a 4–0 win over [[2024 UK Open]] champion [[Dimitri Van den Bergh]].<ref name="LJPW"/> Ninth-seed [[Damon Heta]] was eliminated by a 4–3 scoreline to [[Luke Woodhouse]], being the highest seed to be eliminated in this round despite being the second player to throw a nine-dart finish in the championship.<ref>{{cite web |title=Heta lands nine-darter as PDC world champion Humphries eases through |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/dec/27/damon-heta-lands-nine-darter-as-pdc-world-darts-championship-resumes |website=The Guardian |date=27 December 2024}}</ref> Rydz and Woodhouse were two of seven unseeded players to make it to the fourth round, along with [[Ryan Joyce]], [[Ricardo Pietreczko]], [[Kevin Doets]], [[Jeffrey de Graaf]] and [[Robert Owen (darts player)|Robert Owen]].<ref name="LJPW"/>

Former world champion [[Gerwyn Price]] kept his hopes of regaining his crown alive with a victory over [[Joe Cullen (darts player)|Joe Cullen]], having to come through a deciding leg in the final set despite having previously held a 3–0 lead.<ref>{{cite web |title=World Darts Championship 2025: Gerwyn Price downs Joe Cullen |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/darts/story/_/id/43184589/world-darts-championship-2025-gerwyn-price-downs-joe-cullen |website=ESPN.com |access-date=1 January 2025 |language=en |date=27 December 2024}}</ref> Reigning champion Luke Humphries defeated [[Nick Kenny (darts player)|Nick Kenny]],<ref name="LHGPPW">{{cite web |title=PDC World Darts Championship 2025 results: Luke Humphries, Gerwyn Price & Peter Wright progress |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cg524dyzr71o |website=BBC Sport |access-date=1 January 2025 |date=27 December 2024}}</ref> with former world champions Michael van Gerwen<ref name="LLMVG">{{cite web |title=Luke Littler and Michael van Gerwen progress in PDC World Championships |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/dec/28/pdc-world-darts-saturday-luke-littler-van-gerwen-aspinall |website=The Observer |access-date=1 January 2025 |date=28 December 2024}}</ref> and Peter Wright,<ref name="LHGPPW"/> and pre-tournament favourite [[Luke Littler]] also winning their third round matches.<ref name="LLMVG"/>

===Fourth round===
Reigning champion [[Luke Humphries]]'s campaign ended in the fourth round after he was defeated 4–1 by Peter Wright.<ref name="LJPW">{{cite web |last1=Matthews |first1=Callum |last2=Drury |first2=Sam |title=PDC World Darts Championship 2025 results: Luke Humphries knocked out by Peter Wright |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cnv3pmpdl16o |website=[[BBC Sport]] |access-date=1 January 2025 |date=29 December 2024}}</ref> Wright's fellow former world champions also qualified, with Gerwyn Price knocking out fellow Welshman [[Jonny Clayton]] 4–2<ref name="LJPW"/> and [[Michael van Gerwen]] eliminating Jeffrey de Graaf by the same scoreline.<ref name="29DecGuar">{{cite web |last1=Liew |first1=Jonathan |date=31 December 2024 |title=Luke Littler comes up with killer finish to deny Ryan Joyce a dramatic upset |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2024/dec/30/van-gerwen-holds-off-de-graaf-comeback-to-seal-quarter-final-spot-pdc-world-darts |website=[[The Guardian]] |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref> Callan Rydz was the only unseeded player to reach the quarter-final, beating Robert Owen 4–3 to reach the second quarter final of his career.<ref name="29DecGuar"/>

Two other matches went the full seven sets, Luke Littler defeating [[Ryan Joyce]] to reach his second quarter-final from two attempts and [[Chris Dobey]] beating [[Kevin Doets]] to get through the fourth round for the third consecutive year. The remaining matches were won by scorelines of 4–0, with [[2024 Masters (darts)|2024 Masters]] champion [[Stephen Bunting]] eliminating [[Luke Woodhouse]] and [[Nathan Aspinall]] conquering a distinctly below-par [[Ricardo Pietreczko]].<ref name="29DecGuar"/>

===Quarter-finals===
The quarter-finals were held on [[New Year's Day]] 2025. Chris Dobey played Gerwyn Price in the first quarter-final. Price won the first two sets before Dobey took control, winning four sets in a row to go one away. Price won the seventh set after Dobey missed five darts to win the match, but Dobey won the eighth set 3–1 to qualify for the first world championship semi-final of his career. In the second quarter-final Michael van Gerwen played Callan Rydz. After five sets, which were all won by the player throwing first, Van Gerwen held a 3–2 lead, before breaking Rydz's throw in the third leg of the sixth set on his way to a 3–1 victory in that set to go two sets up. Rydz, who had earlier missed a dart to win the fifth set, managed to win the seventh set against the Van Gerwen throw, but missed two darts in the deciding leg of the eighth set, allowing Van Gerwen to win that set and the match to qualify for the world championship semi-final for the ninth time of his career.<ref name="QF"/>

Stephen Bunting played Peter Wright in the third quarter-final. Bunting won the first four sets, including winning the fourth set 3–0 against the throw, before Wright came back and won sets five and six. Bunting secured the match victory with a 3–0 win in the seventh set to reach the second PDC world championship semi-final of his career. In the final quarter-final Luke Littler took on Nathan Aspinall. Littler got off to a strong start, winning the first set with three consecutive twelve-darters, and took the second set against the Aspinall throw. Aspinall broke back in the third set, but a Littler eleven-darter in the deciding leg of the fourth set restored the two-set advantage. Littler won the fifth set to go one away, and while Aspinall did manage to take the sixth set, another 3–0 scoreline in the seventh set ensured Littler would qualify for the semi-final for the second consecutive year.<ref name="QF">{{cite web |last1=Keogh |first1=Francis |last2=Drury |first2=Sam |date=1 January 2025 |title=PDC World Darts Championship 2025 results: Luke Littler beats Nathan Aspinall to set up Stephen Bunting semi-final |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/cn9gnxlw87do |website=BBC Sport |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref>

===Semi-finals===
In the first semi-final, Michael van Gerwen played Chris Dobey. Van Gerwen won the first set against the throw, and took advantage of three missed darts from Dobey to also take the second. Dobey got off the mark with a 3–0 win in the third set, including a 170 checkout in the second leg, but Van Gerwen won the fourth set by the same scoreline to re-gain the two set advantage before breaking in the fifth set. Van Gerwen took the next two sets to complete a 6–1 victory to reach his seventh world championship final. Luke Littler took on Stephen Bunting in the other semi-final. Littler won the first set 3–2, before breaking in second set 3–1. The same 3–2, 3–1 pattern was repeated in the next two sets, giving Littler a 4–0 lead. Bunting broke back the fifth set with a 3–1 win, but missed 3 to win the sixth set, which Littler won 3–2 to go one away. With a 3–1 win in the seventh set Littler also completed a 6–1 win to reach the world championship final for the second time.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Keogh |first1=Frank |title=Luke Littler to face Michael van Gerwen in PDC World Darts Championship final |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/c3vr045zqrlo |website=BBC Sport |access-date=3 January 2025 |date=2 January 2025}}</ref>

===Final===
Luke Littler took on Michael van Gerwen in the final, held on Friday January 3. Van Gerwen threw first in the opening set, but missed three darts, and lost a fourth after busting his score, to win the first leg, enabling Littler to break and eventually win the set 3–1. Littler won the second set 3–0. In the second leg of the third set Van Gerwen missed three darts to break, and Littler won that leg and the next two to go 3–0 up in the match. The third leg of the fourth set saw Van Gerwen break the Littler throw for the first time in the match – with Littler having missed two darts for the set – but in the next leg van Gerwen missed more darts and Littler broke again to cross the halfway mark towards the championship.<ref name=GuardianFinal>{{Cite web |last=Liew |first=Jonathan |date=3 January 2025 |title=Luke Littler thrashes Michael van Gerwen to claim historic world title |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2025/jan/03/luke-littler-thrashes-michael-van-gerwen-to-claim-historic-world-title |website=[[The Guardian]]}}</ref>

Van Gerwen got off the mark with a 3–1 win in the fifth set, but Littler held the sixth set to go two sets away from victory. Van Gerwen took the seventh set 3–2, but again Littler won the next set. Van Gerwen won the ninth set, but Littler broke in the second leg of the next set to set himself up to throw for the match, and a fourteen-dart leg finished on double 16 wrapped up the world championship for Littler. Aged 17, Littler became the youngest world champion in PDC history, beating the record previously held by Van Gerwen who was 24 years old when he triumphed in 2014.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Cartwright |first1=Phil |last2=Higham |first2=Paul |last3=Keogh |first3=Frank |date=3 January 2025 |title=World Darts Championship Final 2025 LIVE: Luke Littler vs Michael van Gerwen – score, results & stats |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/live/c4gwz153e7et |website=BBC Sport |access-date=3 January 2025}}</ref><ref name=HighamFinal>{{cite web |last=Higham |first=Paul |date=3 January 2025 |title=Teenager Littler demolishes Van Gerwen to claim historic world title |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/darts/articles/clyx7403y9ko |website=BBC Sport |access-date=3 January 2025}}</ref>
